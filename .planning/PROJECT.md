# Trend Street — Project Overview

## Vision
Trend Street ("The Style Hub") is a high-fashion menswear e-commerce experience designed with high editorial aesthetic standards, interactive 3D WebGL showroom capabilities, complete customer purchase journeys, an administrative management suite with privileged access registration, and robust AI & search engine visibility.
